Why we build slowly

"A chair should outlive the reason you bought it."

Most furniture is engineered for the showroom, not the decade. We start from the opposite question: what joint, what grain, what proportion will still be right in twenty years?

Every piece is hand-finished in our workshop and shipped with a repair kit and a standing offer — send it back, we'll restore it, for as long as you own it.

— The Workshop, Astoria OR

From board
to object

The same three stages, every piece, no matter the order size.

01 — Sourced

Selected by grain

Kiln-dried hardwood chosen board by board for grain direction and stability, not yield.

02 — Joined

Cut by hand

Mortise-and-tenon and dovetail joinery, no fasteners, no adhesive shortcuts.

03 — Finished

Oiled, not sealed

A hardwax oil finish that ages with use and can always be sanded back and renewed.
